Controls Guide

Desktop (Keyboard & Mouse)

  • W A S D: Move
  • Space: Jump / Swim Up (Double-tap in Creative to Fly)
  • Shift: Sneak (prevents edge fall) / Fly Down
  • Ctrl / Double-W: Sprint
  • Mouse: Look around (Click viewport to lock pointer)
  • Left Click: Break Block / Attack Mob
  • Right Click: Place Block / Use Table, Furnace, Chest, Bed / Eat
  • E: Open Inventory & Crafting
  • 1 - 9, 0 / Scroll: Select 10-Slot Hotbar
  • Esc / P: Pause Menu

Mobile / Touchscreen

  • Left Virtual Joystick: Walk & Strafe
  • Right Screen Drag: Look Around
  • Break / Place Buttons: Hold Break to mine, tap Place to build/use
  • Jump / Fly: Jump, Swim, or Ascend/Descend
  • Sneak / Sprint: Toggle crouch or fast run
  • Hotbar & •••: Tap slots or open Inventory

About Blockcraft Lite

Blockcraft Lite is a lightweight, bright 3D voxel sandbox survival and creative game powered by a custom native WebGL engine, procedural Canvas2D pixel textures, procedural Web Audio synthesis, and local IndexedDB world storage.

  • 9 Procedural Biomes: Plains, Forest, Desert, Snow, Mountains, Ocean, Beach, Jungle, Swamp
  • Chunk-based terrain with face culling, frustum culling, caves, ores, trees, and water
  • Full Survival & Creative modes, 2x2 & 3x3 crafting, furnace smelting, chests, farming, beds, and animated mobs
  • Zero external dependencies — runs completely offline
