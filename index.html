ABOUT

games.fun was made by two 13-year-old friends, Samarth and Pratyaksh, and launched in 2026. It started as a simple idea: what if we built the games we love to play, and put them all in one place that anyone can open in a browser, with no downloads, no sign-ups and no waiting?

Samarth is our developer. He writes the code in HTML, CSS and JavaScript and makes the games actually work. Pratyaksh is our designer. He decides how everything looks and feels, from the colors and cards to the menus. Together we made classics like Chess, Snake, Flappy and Tic Tac Toe, then got more ambitious with Hand Cricket, Frontline Battler and Block Craft. Some of them took a lot of tries, a lot of bugs and a lot of late nights to get right, and we learned something new from each one.

We are still learning and still adding more games. Look out for the "Coming soon" cards, we are working on them! If something breaks or you have an idea for what we should build next, please tell us in the Feedback section. Thank you for playing, it really means a lot to us. 💛
